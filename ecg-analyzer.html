Method

From raw signal to heart rate in four steps

  1. Load the recording

    Read one ECG record from PhysioNet. Each record has the signal and its sampling rate.

    Tool: wfdb

  2. Filter the signal

    Remove slow baseline drift and fast noise, like 60 Hz hum from power lines. A band-pass filter keeps the part where the heartbeat is.

    Tool: scipy.signal

  3. Detect the R-peaks

    The R-peak is the tall spike in each heartbeat. Find each one with a height threshold and a minimum time between beats.

    Tool: find_peaks

  4. Calculate heart rate

    Measure the time between two R-peaks. This is the RR interval. Heart rate in bpm = 60 ÷ RR interval in seconds.

    Tool: numpy
